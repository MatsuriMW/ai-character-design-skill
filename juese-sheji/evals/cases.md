# 评测用例

每个用例三组 prompt：A 形容词组（原话直译）、B 主设定组（全身）、C 深入组（主设定 + 特写细节，胸像特写）。
构图统一后缀：A、B 用 `Full body, standing, plain off-white background, even soft light, photorealistic.`；C 用 `Chest-up close-up portrait, plain off-white background, even soft light, photorealistic.`
B、C 是模式 E 跑出来的结果（用例 1 的完整描述见 `../examples/轻佻的男性.md`）；词库更新后可以重跑模式 E 再测。

---

## 1. 轻佻的男性（气质词）

**A**：`A frivolous, flirtatious young East Asian man.`

**B / C**：主设定和特写细节见 `../examples/轻佻的男性.md`。

重点看：中分帘发 + 一缕挂眉尾；衬衫解到第三颗、前塞后不塞；黑珍珠吊坠从领口露出；重心在一条腿、胯歪；C 组的泪痣、杂眉、手指茧。

---

## 2. 清冷的女性（气质词）

一句话（补）：27 岁，古籍修复师，一天说不了几句话，习惯一个人待着。

**A**：`A cold, aloof, ethereal young East Asian woman.`

**B 主设定**：
```
A 27-year-old East Asian woman, slim, long neck, narrow sloping shoulders. Long oval face, high flat forehead, low cheekbones, small pointed chin. Straight thin eyebrows; single-lidded eyes with straight outer corners, slightly wide-set; small straight nose; thin lips, mouth corners naturally level. Cool pale skin. Long straight black hair, center-parted, tucked behind both ears, ends cut blunt at mid-back, no flyaways. Small plain silver stud earrings; a thin black leather strap watch with a small white dial on the left wrist. Oatmeal fine-gauge merino turtleneck, slightly loose, sleeves pushed up to the forearm. Charcoal grey straight wool trousers, ankle length. Black flat leather loafers. Standing upright, chin slightly tucked, hands loosely clasped in front, neutral resting face with lips closed. Keep all of the above identical in every shot.
```

**C 特写细节**：
```
Close-up details: a faint mole on the left side of the neck below the jaw; very fine downy hair on the cheek visible in the light; slightly dry lips; short unpolished fingernails; a faint grey ink stain on the side of the right middle finger; the sweater cuff slightly pilled.
```

重点看：单眼皮会不会被改成双眼皮、冷白皮会不会变成粉嫩、默认表情会不会被加上微笑；C 组的颈部小痣、指侧墨迹。

## 3. 一个老年人（人群 · 「这就是生活」）

一句话（补）：73 岁，退休的纺织厂机修工，每天早上去菜市场，下午在小区门口看人下棋。参照 `../references/styles/人群与身份.md`「城市老年男性」。

**A**：`An old Chinese man.`

**B 主设定**：
```
A 73-year-old Chinese man, short, slightly hunched upper back, thin legs. Long face with sunken cheeks, deep nasolabial folds and forehead lines, large ears with long earlobes, a few overly long white hairs in the eyebrows. Short grey-white hair, clipped close at the sides, thinning on the crown. Faded navy zip-up jacket zipped to mid-chest, over a soft washed-out blue-and-white checked shirt buttoned to the top; dark grey trousers belted high on the waist, no crease left, slightly baggy at the knees. Black traditional cloth shoes with layered white cotton soles and elastic sides, a thin layer of grey dust on the uppers. A dented stainless-steel thermos in one hand, a red-white-blue woven plastic shopping bag in the other; reading glasses tucked into the shirt pocket. Standing with knees slightly bent. Keep all of the above identical in every shot.
```

**C 特写细节**：
```
Close-up details: brown age spots on the cheekbones and the backs of the hands; raised veins on the backs of the hands; thick yellowish fingernails; a calloused, cracked right thumb from years of machine work; one slightly cloudy eye; a small grey stubble patch missed on the jaw; the shirt collar frayed at the fold.
```

重点看：老布鞋上的灰、裤子不再有裤线、布袋和保温杯会不会被洗成「干净的体面老人」；C 组的老年斑、手背青筋、拇指的茧和裂口。

## 4. 有绅士感的中年男人（服装风格）

一句话（补）：48 岁，老牌律所的合伙人，西装都是二十年前在老裁缝那里做的，穿得很旧但很合身。参照 `../references/styles/服装风格.md`「绅装」细分「乡绅」。

**A**：`A gentlemanly, elegant middle-aged East Asian man.`

**B 主设定**：
```
A 48-year-old East Asian man, medium build, straight posture, broad shoulders. Square face, defined jaw, high nose bridge, deep-set eyes with a heavy upper lid, straight brows with a few grey hairs. Short side-parted hair, salt-and-pepper, combed with a little pomade, visible comb lines. Brown herringbone tweed single-breasted two-button jacket with soft natural shoulders, patch pockets and a slightly rolled lapel, sleeves showing about one centimeter of shirt cuff; pale blue oxford button-down shirt; dark green knitted wool tie with a square end, slightly loosened; mid-grey flannel trousers with a single pleat and 4 cm turn-ups, a slight break on the shoes. Dark brown polished leather brogue derbies, creased across the toe. A steel watch with a worn brown leather strap on the left wrist. Standing with one hand in the trouser pocket, the other relaxed. Keep all of the above identical in every shot.
```

**C 特写细节**：
```
Close-up details: fine crow's feet and a vertical frown line between the brows; a small old scar on the chin; the tweed elbows worn smooth and slightly shiny; one horn button with a hairline crack; the shirt collar points slightly curled from washing; clean, short, slightly ridged fingernails.
```

重点看：人字纹粗花呢、贴袋、软肩会不会被改成普通深色西装；裤脚翻边和鞋面的褶；领带是不是针织方头。

## 5. 苦命的中年男人（气质 + 生活痕迹）

一句话（补）：45 岁，在建筑工地扎钢筋二十年，老家有两个上学的孩子。**全篇不写任何情绪词**，测「苦」能不能从形态里长出来。参照 `../references/dimensions/索引.md`「苦命的中年男人」。

**A**：`A miserable, long-suffering middle-aged Chinese man with a bitter face.`

**B 主设定**：
```
A 45-year-old Chinese man, wiry, thick forearms, rounded shoulders, head jutting slightly forward. Long face with sunken cheeks, prominent brow ridge, deep forehead lines and nasolabial folds; sallow uneven skin, deeply sun-tanned on the face and neck. Buzz cut grown out to about 2 cm, greying at the temples, a flattened ring in the hair from a hard hat. Faded grey work jacket with frayed cuffs over a sweat-yellowed white undershirt; olive camouflage work trousers worn thin and pale at the knees; yellow-brown rubber-soled work boots caked with dried cement. A white plastic hard hat with a cracked brim held under one arm. Squatting on his heels, forearms resting on his knees, a cigarette held deep between the fingers. Keep all of the above identical in every shot.
```

**C 特写细节**：
```
Close-up details: thick yellowed calluses across the palm and the base of the thumb; grey dirt under the fingernails; a healed but uneven scar at the left temple; small rust stains on the fingertips; nicotine-yellowed index and middle fingers; slightly yellowed teeth; a pressure mark across the forehead from the hard hat strap.
```

重点看：A 组会不会是一张「演苦」的标准脸（皱眉、嘴角下拉），B 组能不能不演就让人觉得苦；蹲姿、安全帽压痕、水泥渍会不会被洗掉。
